ERMs vs LRAs

LRA: Linear Resonance Actuator, More like a speaker, Apple taptic engine by Camille MOussette

CPX: 10mil amps

Transistor
- 3pins
- take small current as trigger
- Base: connect to micro pin
- Collector: to power, with the load (in this case, motor) in series
- Emitter: to ground
Add a diode, because motor creates voltage, it will send voltage to pin, diode will stop that current
