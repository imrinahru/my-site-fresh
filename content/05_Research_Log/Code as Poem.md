>  Code is not just instruction to machines; it is communication among humans. At its best, it is poetry: concise, clear, and alive with meaning. If we approach our work with that spirit — always stripping away the unnecessary, always seeking purity, always embracing abstraction where it saves us words, always embedding tests to affirm truth, always iterating until clarity emerges — we not only build better software, but also elevate programming into an art.
