- Statex Conductive Thread
- 9V Battery
- Thermochromic or Photochromic Paint
- drop spindle

>  using thermochromic pigments on your yarn, consider using a drop spindle to wind the painted yarn with the statex conductive thread. Because the Statex thread has higher resistance, when you connect the thread to power, it will activate the color change.
