- keep rolling with it
- synthesize different forces
- name the innovation
- recurrent idea

Play Theory
- free movement within a rigid structure (Salen, Zimmerman)

What is design thinking?

- failure of design thinking: solely about expansion of options for development
- lateral thinking
- integrative thinking: bringing two models together
- definition: the ability to combine empathy for the context of a problem, creativity for solutions and insights, rationality to analyze and fit solutions to the context 
- Divergence + Convergence (Brown 2009)
- double diamond: discover, define, develop, deliver
- iterative and non-linear
- Innovation is a successful idea in practice (by whose standard is it successful? meaningful innovation with social value, based on principles, we need to always question that)
	- unintended negative consequences
	- diversity of interest/perspective, inclusion is necessary
	- innovation couples with glory, but sometimes and oftentimes, care, stay with the trouble/struggle is more important than a glorious success
	- interlocking system and cascading result
- Collective Genius
	- Willingness, a sense of community
		- purpose, why we exist, is it worth it
		- shared values, what do we agree is important, support the values of others, I want to take risk, I want to push
		- rules of engagement, how do we expect to be treated, process, hierarchy, time, the way in which we are talked to
