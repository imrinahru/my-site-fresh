## Practice & Studio Work

Digital sample making

The shape needs to be simple enough to translate into 6 samples:

- laser cut piece
- waterjet cut piece
- CNC milled piece 
- vinyl cut piece
- 3D printed piece
- 3D scanned image

Your initial design should be a basic 4" x 4" vector shape.

## Critique & Feedback on Acrylic Laser Cutting

- don't use acetone
- don't use alcohol
- for chips, use nail top/UV raisin

Fake 3D by layering 2 different shapes of acrylics (scissors keychain)
live hinge might work on a certain scale/material
Use cardboard for prototyping

White acrylic has the tendency of melting back after laser cut

Raisin LCD wax printer
	A "Raisin LCD wax printer" refers to an MSLA/LCD 3D printer that uses specialized **castable wax-filled photopolymer resins**, not actual raisins, to create highly detailed jewelry, dental models, and industrial prototypes that can be investment cast into metal

Use of magnet, rare earth magnet (powerful and small)

About gluing
- Raisin bond is tricky to have a fine finish
- Crazy glue, be very precise and well applied
- B7000 jewelry glue

Acrylic marker for finishing acrylic
