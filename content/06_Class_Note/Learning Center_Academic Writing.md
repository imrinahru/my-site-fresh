Annotated bibliographies

- relevance to YOU: eg. this article help to contextualize how AI is used in xx sector, by providing interviews
- 6~7 sentences, 10 lines
- eg. This person is a 
- Summarize what the text is saying, what this text is allowing us to know/do. 
- Discourses: broader field of research
- Include the summary of the text + specific area of interest/relevance

- 30-40 papers
- pre-reading, heading key words (ctl F), Google, narrow down to 18, consider ones relevant, ones interesting.
- Essential: Materials that needs to be there to move your work forward
- Relevant: Supplemental point/data
- Interesting: Fascinating, novel, people, approach, data that spark imagination, they don't necessarily connect to what you want to say
![[Pasted image 20251029125349.png]]
- Organizing sources to see patterns that cross-over sources
- What each source says about an idea, compare and contrast, agreement/disagreement
- Data point: are they citing each other and agreed? Or are they using a different data point and nonetheless arrived at the same conclusion?
- A key term, key idea that are important in the filed
- later on, in essay/theory paper, then either topics you are interested in, questions you have, any points you want to explore, 
- later stage, you will focus on one out of matrix, so stick with key words, relevant ideas data
- make sense of ideas, rather than text, making connections, more utilities later on. What are some ideas that recur, key ideas, key approaches
