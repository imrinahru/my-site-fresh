### Notes  

- The orientation of technology, is it going into the space race, or going down to the earth, like the ancient salt-mine ladder
- Illich: the moment in which technology is not anymore instrument and we become the instruments of the system



- technological instrumentality circulates our body and we are seeing world only through the mediated instruments
- convivial tools: give each person who uses them the greatest opportunity to enrich the environment
- decolonization as a way to find conviviality of tools, rethink technology, recover the complexity
- "The matter and the size of the ladder showing signs of the work of human hands that had a very important role in the making of their world"
- "This world of relationalities and use-values...one can only be humbled by it"
