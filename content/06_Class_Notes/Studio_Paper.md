Things you need:

- Darning/Tapestry needle
- Scissors
- Cord (need to be strong and smooth)
- Ore
- Clump, pinch, clip

Find a piece of cardboard. size 5.5 x 7.5/ 5.5 x 8.5 (deco size)
At the edges, leave a 1/4" and mark, help with aligning the shrank pages
bind at a line 5/8" (1.5cm) from the edge
Do 4 holes
Page itself, 
notes on a small (transparent) paper
- technique
- pulp
- source
- extra materials
- notes: dried with restraint, heat pressed

Align the papers and clump at a place close to where you are stitching
Use an ore to press it through (pushing and turning), punch holes
Remove the template, release it by declumping one side at a time
1.1m long of cord is needed for this binding
Thread it into the needle
Stitching (1, 2, 3, 4)
- Start at hole 3
- Start from inside the book (center), declump one side and start in the middle to thread to the back, tuck the end in side, and clip the end as well.
- Stitch around the spine and across the top (horizontal: 2), then around 2's spine, then go across the bottom (to 1), then around the spine and around the edge, then across the top of the left behinds
- the do the 4 the hole, around the spine, around the corner
- Open up the book to the page where tail is left, pull through
- Tie a overhand knot x 2
- hide them in the spine of the book

garter: leave a good border to accommodate spine
