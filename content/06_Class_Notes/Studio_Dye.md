# Studio: Dye

A coloured substance that chemically bonds with the fibre.
Pigment in dye is soluble in water.

Natural dyes
Avocado skin/seed (yellow, pink)
earth
roots
snails

Cochineal
Walnut shell (black walnut in Ontario, brown)

1847, synthetic dye, mauve purple

Natural Dye Variables
1. the amount in the dye bath (concentration)
2. weight of fibre ratio
3. mordant used
4. additives, modifiers
5. time
6. pH level of water
7. material itself

Whole dyestuff/extract
water
mordant: metallic salts that help the dyestuff bond to the fibre. The color will fade if not used beforehand. It helps creating molecular bond. It also helps even coverage, it can intensify color as well.
scouring soap

Weighing fibres: weight when it's dry, essential for dye calculation

Scouring (washing): fibres can contain natural oil, which impede bonding
