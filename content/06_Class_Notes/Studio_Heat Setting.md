## Heat set textured surfaces

### Shibori technique
- 染め for shapes
1. Tsukidashi kanoko
	- spaced dots binding
	- binging object into fabrics
	- seabeed, smaller , bigger objects will give different results
	- close, regular spacing can be interesting
2. Arashi shibori
	- wrap fabric around a pole
	- larger wrap distance vs smaller wrap distance
	- wrap as tight as possible,
	- then squeeze down along the pole
3. Nui shibori
	- Do running stitch
	- Gather/pull and tie

- Polyester goes to pressure cooker for steaming only
- Wool can be boiled or steamed
- Use strong polyester thread for binding
- Kamisage knot at the beginning 
- surgeon knot at the end (overhand knot but passing twice)
- iron, clamp (wood, clamp, wrapping with thread) to facilitate accordion fold, as precision matters (crisp lines)

Boiling wool using its quality to shrink
- Cut holes and fringes
- boil for 30 min

How to steam
- Add water at bottom
- a plate
- some brown paper
- paper-wrapped fabrics
- avoid touching the side of the cooker
- put heat to high
- wait 15~20 min for temperature to go up
- Lift up the rattle
- steam for 30min
- make sure pressure does not go beyond 15, if it gets above, turn down heat
