## Reflection & Connections
- Start with simple, clean illustration, don't use "hand-drawn-like" brushes, since too many controlling points will make it hard to cut. 
- Most importantly, if there are left out single points remaining in your final work, the vinyl knife will cut across your work to the isolated point
